Local information that matters.
An AI-powered local intelligence network. We collect, understand and organize what's happening in your city — so you can quickly find what actually affects your daily life.

Visit Halifax ↗ City network

Not another
"AI news site".

"AI-generated news" sounds like a content farm — bulk text with no judgment and no local value. Press is built on the opposite idea: AI is the tool, not the product. What matters is filtering, understanding, and local value.

What Press actually does

  • Collect — RSS feeds, public APIs and city sources
  • Understand — classify, deduplicate and merge events
  • Score — rank by real impact on daily life
  • Verify — human review before anything ships
  • Publish — to the web and everywhere readers are

Article generation is not the moat — accumulated data is. Years of grocery prices, fuel trends, road work, housing and policy become a city knowledge base. And a knowledge base becomes something bigger:

Ask Halifax — a city assistant you can actually ask things: "Where is gas cheapest this week?" "What's new with HRCE?" "Which supermarket has milk on sale?"
