How we play

The rules of the playground.

Fast experiments

Small bets, shipped quickly. If an idea can't be tested in weeks, it's too big for Fun.

Fast failure

Most experiments will die. That's the job. Kill fast, keep the learnings, move on.

Creativity first

No revenue targets at the start. If players love it, monetization can come later — or it graduates to Host.

Flagship concept

Halifax Planet:
a reality-powered
virtual city.

What if a game city ran on real city data?

The idea

  • You get an avatar, a home, levels, quests and achievements in a virtual Halifax
  • Real weather — when it snows in Halifax, it snows on the Planet
  • Real fuel prices — energy costs in-game move with the real world
  • Real news & events — powered by AIMeta Press data

Reality in, play out.

The Planet turns Press data into play — and play into community. It's the missing link between information and belonging.

Status: concept stage. We're sketching, not shipping — yet.
